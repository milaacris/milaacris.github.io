CSV Data Processor

Instructions:

Upload a CSV file with the following structure:

  • Year - The year (e.g., 2023, 2024)
  • Month - The month number (1-12)
  • Product - Product SKU (e.g., SKU1, SKU2)
  • Value QTY - Total quantity for the month

The processor will distribute quantities evenly across all days of each month.
